Questions

Common questions

Is a video consultation safe and allowed?

Yes. India's Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020) allow registered doctors to consult by video. We follow them for consent, identity checks, records and prescriptions.

What do I need?

A smartphone with a camera and internet, your recent reports, and a list of the medicines you take.

Can the doctor prescribe medicines?

Yes, within the telemedicine rules. Some medicines cannot be prescribed on a first video consultation; the doctor will tell you if an in-person visit is needed.

Can Carevya help in an emergency?

No. In an emergency, call 108 for an ambulance or 112, and go to the nearest hospital.
