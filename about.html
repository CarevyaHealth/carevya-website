About Carevya Health

Specialist care should not depend on where you live.

Carevya Health was started by a doctor and a healthcare strategist who saw the same problem again and again: patients in smaller towns travelling for hours, losing a day's work, just to get one specialist's opinion. We bring that opinion to them.

Our mission

The right specialist, the right decision, close to home

✓

For patients and families, Carevya Care offers video consultations for second opinions, report reviews and long-term conditions.

✓

For hospitals, clinics and OHCs, Carevya Connect adds same-day specialist opinions, with the site's own doctor examining the patient.

✓

For communities, we work with CSR and district programmes to bring specialists to primary health centres.

How we work

Five promises we keep

Doctors

Registered specialists only

Every consultant is registered with the National Medical Commission or a State Medical Council.

Rules

Telemedicine guidelines followed

Consent, identity checks, records and prescriptions follow India's Telemedicine Practice Guidelines (2020).

Privacy

Your records stay private

Reports and consultation notes are shared only with the doctors treating you.

Choice

The patient decides

When hospital care is needed, we explain the options. We never take referral commissions.

Safety

Honest about limits

We do not handle emergencies, and we tell you when an in-person visit is needed.
